United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) Branding Guidelines

This material is subject to the United Nations guidelines for the use of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) Logo, Colour Wheel and 17 Icons.

For informational, non-commercial use, the United Nations permits use of the SDG branding without prior permission, subject to the Sustainable Development Goals Guidelines.

Only United Nations System entities may use the SDG Logo with the United Nations emblem. Entities outside the United Nations System must use the version without the United Nations emblem.

The use of the SDG branding must not imply endorsement by the United Nations of any products, services or activities.

The following statement must be included in online and printed materials:

"The content of this publication has not been approved by the United Nations and does not reflect the views of the United Nations or its officials or Member States."
